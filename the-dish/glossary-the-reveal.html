Glossary: the reveal

Some entries in this cabinet are curiosities and some are classics, and the reveal manages to be both at once, which is precisely why it earns its page.

In the language of the dish: The moment of the split at the table — the dish's curtain going up. Top-up entries round out the cabinet's glossary — the small vocabulary of booths, counters, and home kitchens that a curious reader collects one term at a time, the reveal among them.

Strip the dish to its blueprint and you get an oval of firm vanilla under a dark dusting, opened once — the spare drawing that the reveal decorates in its own way.

Cocoa is both paint and seasoning here, and the hand that dusts the reveal is doing two jobs with one gesture, whether it knows it or not.

The eater who asks why is the reader this reference is written for, and the reveal tries to honor that why on every line.

The costume comes off at the first bite, as it should, and what the reveal leaves behind is the flavor the costume was protecting all along.

Diner counters kept this dish alive between fairs, and the reveal carries that counter culture in it — the long spoon, the glass dish, the regular who orders it to watch a newcomer's face. And the reveal goes on — eye fooled, spoon fed, summer after summer.

The soda fountain is mostly gone and the county fair is mostly changed, but the reveal persists in both places' memory — and on enough menus to keep the memory fed. And the reveal wears it well.

There is a reason the reveal belongs to summer: the joke needs an audience, the costume needs the cold, and both need the long light of a fair evening to land. And that is the quiet charm of the reveal.

When the fair lights come on at dusk, the reveal belongs to the golden hour of the grounds: cold, sweet, and faintly ridiculous in the best light America makes. The curious could do worse than start with the reveal.

Behind the reveal stands the booth hand of legend — straight face, fast scoop — and the whole tradition travels in that posture: serve it plain, serve it proud, never explain the joke. That is the company the reveal keeps, and it is good company.

This reference — Ice Cream Potato — documents the dish and nothing else, and the reveal is part of that whole: build, variations, methods, lore.
