History & Culture

The photography instinct

What the midway knew, and the soda fountain agreed with, is that appetite and laughter are neighbors — and the photography instinct has lived on that border its whole life.

Long before phones, fairgoers photographed the dish — it is in family albums across the Northwest, held up beside prize ribbons and midway lights. The instinct is the dish's oldest review: nobody photographs a dessert that didn't delight them first.

At the center of the cabinet stands the plain skin-on scoop, patient as a museum piece, while the photography instinct and its cousins circle it like exhibits around a founder.

A novelty asks to be looked at; a classic asks to be eaten; and the photography instinct, at its best, manages to be asked both questions at once.

Curiosity is the only ticket this cabinet charges, and the photography instinct collects it gladly at the door.

So the record stands on the photography instinct: a dessert that dresses as dinner, a joke that eats like a classic, a potato that never was.

A costume dessert succeeds when the eater plays along, and the photography instinct has never lacked for players: generations have happily pretended, at first sight, that dinner had arrived early. The curious could do worse than start with the photography instinct.

Every summer, somewhere, a first-timer points at the photography instinct and laughs out loud — and that laugh, repeated for a century, is the dish's true recipe. That is the pleasure of the photography instinct, plainly stated.

The humor is the costume; underneath it, the photography instinct argues for humble things taken seriously — one honest scoop, carefully dressed, carrying a century of summers. That is the company the photography instinct keeps, and it is good company.

The dish's home turf is potato country, and the connection is the whole point of the photography instinct: a dessert that honors the russet by impersonating it, served in the places where the real thing built the local pride. It is one more reason the photography instinct rewards the curious.

Some novelties age into embarrassments; the photography instinct aged into a tradition, because underneath the costume there was always a genuinely good dessert keeping the joke honest. It is a story told by the photography instinct without a single word.

The Ice Cream Potato reference keeps the photography instinct on the record as one face of a single dish — classic build, variations, methods, and lore together.
