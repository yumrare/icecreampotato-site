Lore: the borrowed booth's drift

The old fairs sold wonder by the ticket, and tucked into that economy of wonder, the borrowed booth's drift sold the cheapest astonishment on the grounds: a potato that was not a potato.

A standing entry in the dish's lore: the borrowed booth's drift. When a distant fair borrows the dish, the build drifts — a sauce here, a size there — and the drift becomes that fair's own tradition. Lore entries are labeled lore — the dish's living tradition as its makers and eaters tell it — and the reference keeps them gladly, because a dish this loved is partly made of its stories; the borrowed booth's drift is one of the stories it keeps best.
