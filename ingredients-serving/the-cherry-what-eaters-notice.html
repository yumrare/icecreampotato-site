The cherry: what eaters notice

Nostalgia is not the whole story of the cherry, but it is the doorway — behind it stands a small, sturdy craft of freezing, dusting, and splitting that this entry takes seriously.

the cherry is noticed by eaters long before it is named by them. Its stem, when kept, is the photographer's friend — the one vertical line in the dish's composition. Quiet work, in every bite of the classic: that is what the cherry does.

Vanilla is the flesh, cocoa is the skin, whipped cream plays the sour cream — the casting never changes, and the cherry is one more performance by the same small company of players.

Restraint is the house style: one split, one dollop, nothing shouting — and the cherry shows what restraint looks like when it is done with affection.

The reference exists because a dish this playful deserves a serious home, and the cherry gets its entry the way every variation and mention does: documented in full, and gladly.
