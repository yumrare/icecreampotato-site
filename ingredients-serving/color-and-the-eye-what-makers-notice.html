Color and the eye: what makers notice

Makers learn color and the eye by its behavior, not its chemistry. The eye reads the dish before the tongue does: brown mottling at potato scale, a pale interior, a white dollop. The brain files 'baked potato' from color and shape long before it consults the menu. On the troubleshooting pages, color and the eye leaves its fingerprints everywhere.
